    How it works

    About this site

    What it does

    Food Safety Watch gathers food recalls, allergy alerts, outbreak notices and consumer protection news from official public feeds and puts them on one page that you can search and filter.

    The trust rule

    The site only shows links on government or United Nations web domains, such as .gov, .gov.uk, .nhs.uk, .gov.in, canada.ca, europa.eu, who.int and fao.org. The collector checks every link and drops any item from another domain.

    The every country directory covers all 194 WHO member states. Each country links to its WHO country office. Where possible, it also links to a national food safety, health or consumer protection authority. Those links are checked automatically (build_countries.py), and any site that doesn't respond, shows an error, or is under maintenance is left out rather than guessed.

    How it stays current

    A small script (fetch_feeds.py) downloads each agency's public RSS feed or open-data API and saves the results. Run it again, or schedule it, to refresh the page. The time of the last update is shown at the top of the feed.

    Important

    This is an independent reading aid. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the agencies listed. Always check the original notice on the agency's site before acting on it. If you have eaten a recalled product and feel unwell, contact a doctor or your local health service.
